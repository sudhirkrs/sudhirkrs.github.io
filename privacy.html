Privacy Policy

History of Kings publishing tool · Last updated 12 November 2026

This policy covers the History of Kings publishing tool, a private application that the owner of the History of Kings YouTube channel uses to manage that channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public, and only the channel owner signs in to it.

What the tool accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for these YouTube permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload the channel's own episodes and thumbnails.
  • Manage the YouTube account (youtube.force-ssl): to schedule those videos, edit their titles and descriptions, add captions, add them to playlists, and post the channel's own first comment.
  • Read YouTube Analytics and channel data (yt-analytics.readonly, youtube.readonly): to read the channel's own view and watch-time reports and video list.

How the data is used

  • Data is used only to publish and manage videos on the History of Kings channel and to read its own reports.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's computer. It is not uploaded anywhere.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The tool does not read or store data about viewers or other YouTube users beyond the public totals in the channel's own reports.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses YouTube API Services, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also ends the tool's access.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the History of Kings YouTube channel.
