History of Kings

Documentary stories of the great rulers of India, Asia and Europe.

History of Kings is a history channel. Each long episode tells the life of one ruler, from Ashoka and Akbar to Genghis Khan, Charlemagne and Peter the Great, using historical sources, maps and pictures from Wikimedia Commons. Short clips each day pick out one moment or one open question from those stories.
